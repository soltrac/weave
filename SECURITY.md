# Security Policy

## Supported versions

Weave is pre-1.0. Security fixes land on `main` and ship in the next release of
the `@weaveio/*` packages. Only the latest published release is supported.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue for security problems.

Report vulnerabilities privately through GitHub:
[Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/weave-io/weave/security/advisories/new).

Include what you can of:

- the affected package(s) and version(s)
- steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
- the impact you expect

We aim to acknowledge reports within a few business days and will keep you
updated while we work on a fix. Once a fix is released, we publish an advisory
and credit the reporter unless you ask us not to.
